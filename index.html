[ Case study / 01 ]

01

StageRing

A tactile ring for introverts experiencing glossophobia - the fear of public speaking.

The challenge

How might we create a comforting product that helps reduce anxiety during live presentations, without making the wearer feel more visible?

Touch is a discreet language. StageRing turns nervous energy into a personal ritual through interchangeable smooth, neutral, and rough textures.

[ Case study / 02 ]

02

Enclosure

A speculative fuel station reimagined as a living public connector.

The proposition

Rather than an anonymous stopover, Enclosure makes the fuel station an active site for conversation - connecting mobility, community, green space, and energy awareness.

The roof is conceived as a breathable field of solids and voids, allowing air, light, rainwater harvesting, and photovoltaic technology to shape a more regenerative everyday infrastructure.

[ Case study / 03 ]

03

$5.50*n

An installation about the meaning that gathers in repetition.

The reflection

Every day I eat $5.50 worth of noodles at the same place, at the same time. A routine that seems dull became the starting point for a work about slowness, accumulation, and attention.

One toothpick changes almost nothing. Hundreds of small, repeated actions slowly become a structure - a visual record of invisible growth.
